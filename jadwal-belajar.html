Template jadwal belajar mingguan yang bisa kamu tiru

Contoh jadwal belajar mingguan: blok waktu harian, sesi hafalan singkat, dan review di akhir pekan.

Kenapa perlu jadwal belajar

Tanpa jadwal, belajar menunggu mood — dan mood jarang datang. Dengan jadwal, otakmu tahu kapan waktunya fokus, dan kamu tidak perlu memutuskan ulang setiap hari.

Contoh jadwalnya

  1. Senin–Jumat, 16.00–16.45: kerjakan PR dan tugas sekolah.
  2. Senin–Jumat, 19.00–19.30: hafalan dengan flashcard.
  3. Sabtu, 09.00–10.00: review materi minggu ini.
  4. Minggu: libur. Istirahat juga bagian dari jadwal.

Sesuaikan jamnya dengan jadwal sekolahmu — yang penting polanya: belajar, hafalan singkat, lalu review.

Atur blok waktunya

Mulai dari blok 25 menit kalau 45 menit terasa berat. Tambah pelan-pelan tiap minggu. Sisipkan jeda 5 menit di antara blok supaya fokusmu pulih.

Jadwalkan review, bukan cuma materi baru

Banyak yang lupa menjadwalkan pengulangan. Satu sesi review di akhir pekan mencegah materi minggu ini hilang begitu saja.

A weekly study schedule template you can copy

A sample weekly study schedule: daily time blocks, short memorization sessions, and a weekend review.

Why you need a study schedule

Without a schedule, studying waits for motivation — and motivation rarely shows up. With a schedule, your brain knows when it is time to focus, and you stop renegotiating with yourself every day.

The sample schedule

  1. Monday–Friday, 4:00–4:45 PM: do homework and school assignments.
  2. Monday–Friday, 7:00–7:30 PM: memorization with flashcards.
  3. Saturday, 9:00–10:00 AM: review this week's material.
  4. Sunday: off. Rest is part of the schedule too.

Adjust the hours to your school timetable — what matters is the pattern: study, short memorization, then review.

Set your time blocks

Start with 25-minute blocks if 45 feels heavy. Add more each week. Take a 5-minute break between blocks so your focus recovers.

Schedule review, not just new material

Many people forget to schedule repetition. One weekend review session keeps this week's material from fading away.

Keep going with the same subject

Connect your classes, assignments, and practice cards through the same subject.
