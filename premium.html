Explore plans in the app

Open Profile → Premium to see available features, subscription periods, and prices for your account. Review the Google Play details before completing a purchase.

Open Google Play

Restore your subscription

  1. Use the Google Play account used to purchase and sign in with the associated Rancana account.
  2. Connect to the internet and open Profile → Premium.
  3. Choose Restore purchase and wait for verification.
  4. If no purchase is found, check your subscription status and account in Google Play. If the issue continues, contact the Rancana team.

Restore purchase checks subscription access; it does not restore a planner backup.

Manage your subscription

What's free?

Subjects, schedules, to-dos, typed flashcards and files are free forever, with no limits. You can also try timetable and flashcard scans from photos on the Free plan, within a set limit.

Does an older Premium subscription include advanced study features?

Some older Premium subscriptions include advanced study features. Check your account access in Profile → Premium.

How do I cancel renewal?

Open Subscriptions in Google Play, select Rancana, and follow the cancellation steps. Make sure you are using the correct Google Play account. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

When does access end after cancellation?

For a canceled subscription, access continues until the paid period ends. Check the date in Google Play. Read the official Google Play guide.

What happens when Premium+ ends?

All your data stays and works as usual. You go back to the Free plan, which still has no limits on subjects, schedules, to-dos, typed flashcards and files.

Already on Premium?

Your Premium plan stays active until its period ends.

Billing and cancellation guidance: Google Play Help. For app issues, visit Help & Contact.

Buka Google Play

Sudah berlangganan? Pulihkan akses langganan

  1. Pakai akun Google Play yang kamu gunakan untuk berlangganan, lalu masuk dengan akun Rancana yang terkait.
  2. Pastikan perangkatmu terhubung ke internet, lalu buka Profil → Rancana Premium.
  3. Ketuk Restore purchase untuk memeriksa akses langgananmu, lalu tunggu sampai prosesnya selesai.
  4. Pembelian belum ditemukan? Cek status langganan dan akunmu di Google Play. Kalau masih ada kendala, hubungi kami.

Fitur Restore purchase memeriksa akses langgananmu. Fitur ini tidak memulihkan data pelajaran, tugas, jadwal, atau flashcard dari cadangan.

Ada pertanyaan soal langganan?

Apa saja yang gratis?

Mata pelajaran, jadwal, to-do, flashcard yang kamu ketik, dan file bisa kamu pakai gratis selamanya, tanpa batas. Scan jadwal dan flashcard dari foto juga bisa dicoba di paket Gratis, dengan batas tertentu.

Kalau punya langganan Premium versi lama, dapat fitur belajar lanjutan juga?

Sebagian langganan Premium versi lama sudah mencakup fitur belajar lanjutan. Cek fitur yang bisa kamu pakai di Profil → Rancana Premium.

Mau berhenti berlangganan, caranya gimana?

Buka Langganan di Google Play, pilih Rancana, lalu ikuti langkah pembatalannya. Pastikan kamu pakai akun Google Play yang benar. Menghapus aplikasi tidak membatalkan langganan, ya.

Kalau dibatalkan, Premium bisa dipakai sampai kapan?

Setelah kamu membatalkan langganan, akses Premium tetap aktif sampai akhir periode yang sudah dibayar. Cek tanggal berakhirnya di Google Play. Untuk informasi lebih lanjut, baca panduan resmi Google Play.

Kalau Premium+ berakhir, gimana?

Semua datamu tetap ada dan bisa dipakai seperti biasa. Kamu kembali ke paket Gratis, yang tetap tanpa batas untuk mata pelajaran, jadwal, to-do, flashcard ketik, dan file.

Sudah langganan Premium?

Paket Premium kamu tetap aktif sampai periodenya berakhir.
