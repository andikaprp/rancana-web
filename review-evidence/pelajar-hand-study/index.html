Start from the audience mascots.

Pelajar Ranca and Mahasiswa Cana are the canonical references. First compare the original construction with the rejected hands, then review one restrained cupped-hand greeting study. These are source references, not instructions to reuse one pose everywhere.

Pelajar Ranca

  • Soft, rounded green lobes; slight three-quarter posture, backpack to the left.
  • Open white bean eyes with green cutouts, small brows, cheerful open smile.
  • Roomy rounded palm, three short stacked finger curves, open wrist join and one flowing arm.

Mahasiswa Cana

  • Distinct asymmetric pink lobes and a tilted graduation cap; confident upward angle.
  • Half-lidded eyes, one raised brow and a small curved smile—not Ranca’s expression in pink.
  • Pointing fingers extend from a rounded palm; the other digits stay compact. Arm and fingers share the same line weight.

Both originals are unchanged, single-frame raster assets. The current main’s audience images had no CSS, Web Animation, or pixel motion in the local observation. The live site was inaccessible here, so no live-site playback claim is made.

Where the rejected construction went wrong

The actions differ, but the drawing language should agree. The original hand has generous internal space, compact curves and a flowing attachment. The revision crowded the palm with deep notches and closed it at the wrist.

What this study changes

Start with a centerline trace of the Pelajar hand. Ease its compact finger curves open just a little, raise the wrist, give two small wrist-led waves, and settle.

The hand stays an open contour with a generous palm. One continuous arm joins the wrist; its shoulder anchor remains fixed. Fingers move together, with no independent wiggling or closed glove base.

This is a labeled vector reconstruction and a proposed motion, not original animation or pixel-identical artwork. The overlay allows the trace to be checked against the raster. It does not replace a body, face, audience asset, or full scene. Placement on a complete mascot still needs review.
