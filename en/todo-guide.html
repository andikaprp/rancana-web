How to create and manage a to-do list in Rancana

Record an assignment, connect it to a subject, and track it through to completion.

Start with a subject

Open Rancana on Android. In Subjects, choose Add subject, enter a title and description, then confirm. For example, create “Mathematics”. This subject connects your schedules, to-dos, and flashcards.

Add your first task

  1. Open your subject, choose the To-do tab, then Add To-do.
  2. Enter a clear title, such as “Finish chapter 2 exercises”. Add a description: “Questions 1–5, submit through class”. Both fields are required.
  3. Choose the subject. You can also change the emoji.
  4. If the task has a deadline, enable the deadline option and select a date and time. Tasks can also have no deadline.
  5. If needed, enable the reminder option and choose how long before the deadline to be reminded. Allow Android notifications for reminders to appear.
  6. Tap Add to-do, then confirm.

A task you can check at a glance

Finish chapter 2 exercises

Description: Questions 1–5, submit through class.
Subject: Mathematics
Example deadline: Friday, 16:00

This date and time are examples. Use the deadline your teacher or lecturer gave you.

Manage tasks after adding them

In the to-do list, filter by All, Active, Missed, or Completed. Once a task is done, mark it complete using its status button. Use the edit icon to update details and save changes. The delete icon asks for confirmation.

If the add button is disabled

Fill in the title, description, and subject. An enabled deadline needs both a date and time; an enabled reminder needs a selected reminder time too. The Free plan allows 10 active to-dos. Mark tasks complete when you have actually finished them.

Keep going with the same subject

Connect your classes, assignments, and practice cards through the same subject.
