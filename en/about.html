About Rancana

Rancana is a companion for everyday studies, bringing subjects, assignments, class schedules, and flashcards together for school and college students. Built by Andika Pramudya, a student and product designer, it grew from his own everyday study needs.

Our mission

Make everyday studying feel easier.

Know what needs doing today, prepare assignments before their deadlines, and arrive at class ready. Schedules and tasks stay available even when your notebook is left behind.

Prepare for today

Review with purpose

Practice with flashcards and make time to focus.

A clear place to plan, prepare, and review. Rancana helps students keep track of what comes next without adding more confusion.

Use flashcards to practice and Study Mode to track study time. Rancana supports purposeful screen time alongside pen and paper, with the aim of becoming a helpful companion for everyday studies.
