How to fill in your schedule

  1. Add activities with fixed times first. Start with classes, practical sessions, work and meetings. Check the location or class link too.
  2. Leave time for travel, meals and rest. Avoid scheduling back-to-back activities if you still need to change locations.
  3. Write assignment deadlines in a separate column. The time you work on an assignment and its submission deadline serve different purposes.
  4. Break assignments into clear steps. Replace “work on the paper” with “find 3 sources”, “make an outline” or “write the introduction”.
  5. Choose study time that is actually available. Start with one small activity that you can finish in that slot.
  6. Review the schedule at the end of the week. Move unfinished work and adjust any periods that turned out to be too crowded.

When your schedule changes

If a class changes time, update it first. Then check whether your travel and study time need to move too. If two deadlines are close together, break the work down earlier and mark the parts to finish first.

You can also add a “spare time” row for activities that move. You do not have to fill every hour.

Before a new week starts

  • Class times and locations match the latest information
  • Every assignment has a clear next step
  • Submission deadlines are recorded
  • There is time for travel, meals and rest
  • The workload is realistic for your circumstances this week
