WHO’S RANCANA FOR?

Still in school, already in college, or preparing for exams?

From junior high and senior high to vocational school and college, or while you prepare for exams, Rancana can be your study companion.

SCHOOL STUDENTS

For junior high, senior high and vocational students

Use Rancana to organize your class schedule and homework to-do list. Group assignments by subject, make flashcards to review what you are learning, turn on reminders, and start a 10-minute study session.

COLLEGE STUDENTS

For you at college

From your first semester to your last, the Rancana study app helps you organize assignments, your college class schedule, and your study sessions.

EXAM STUDENTS

For students preparing for exams

Planning a big exam? Keep your schedule, review material, and flashcards in one place, turn on reminders, start a 10-minute study session, or share your exam plan as an image.

Want to know more about Rancana?

Where can I get Rancana?

You can get it on Google Play for Android in 9 countries: Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, the Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam, Cambodia, Laos, and Myanmar. Just tap “Get it on Google Play” on this page.

Can I start for free?

Yes! You can start free. If you want Premium, check its features and pricing in the app first.

Can I use it at school or college?

Yes. Use subjects for school or courses for college, then add tasks, class schedules, and flashcards. If you are preparing for an exam, you can keep what you need to review and plan your study sessions here too.

What can I organize in Rancana?

Subjects, tasks, class schedules, and flashcards. There are also reminders for class schedules and tasks, a 10-minute study session in Study Mode, plus a study timer and reports to track your study time.

Can I share my exam plan?

Yes. You can share your exam plan as an image for a post or a story, right from the app.

Who can I contact for help?

Just email help@rancana.id. Tell us what’s wrong and which app version you use, so we can help.
