Ranca's reference has soft rounded green lobes, a slight three-quarter posture, open white bean eyes with green cutouts, small brows and a cheerful open smile. Cana has a separate asymmetric pink lobe profile, half-lidded eyes, one raised brow and a small curved smile; her expression is deliberately more sassy. Keep the smooth shading and simple white facial vocabulary. Do not add realistic irises, teeth, lashes or body outlines.

Hands have broad, rounded palm space, short rounded finger arcs, consistent arm/finger line weight, and a flowing open wrist connection. A new gesture may open or turn a hand, but should keep that construction. Avoid deep narrow notches, long splayed generic glove fingers, heavy closed cuffs, crowded palm interiors, and a thicker arm stub attached to a disconnected hand. Test the whole arm-to-wrist curve as the hand turns, not only the finger interpolation.

These images guide anatomy and personality, not an unchanging grip/pointing pose for every context. In particular, sunglasses Cana should have her own posture and action; changing an accessory on the mahasiswa pointing pose is insufficient.
